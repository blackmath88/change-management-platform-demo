Overview / Command Center
Context-aware AI for change practitioners

An AI-ready change workspace.

Five units for building the change case, plus a sixth diagnostic for situations where positions will not converge. Each unit generates framework-aware prompts you take to Claude, ChatGPT, or your institution's AI tool. The platform doesn't embed a chatbot — it shapes the conversation.

How AI fits in

An AI-ready workspace, not an AI app

This platform does not embed a chatbot. Each unit generates structured, framework-aware prompts from your case data — tailored to that unit's specific change-management logic. You copy the prompt into your preferred AI tool: ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI your institution approves.

The AI integration is technically lightweight but pedagogically specific: the prompts reflect the framework of each unit, so AI interaction reinforces the course methodology rather than distracting from it.
